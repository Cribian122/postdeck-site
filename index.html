What PostDeck does

  • It cuts long videos and stream recordings into short vertical clips, with captions and edit styles.
  • It uploads the clips to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Bluesky, now or at a scheduled time.
  • It runs on your own computer. It has no server and no user accounts.

From a stream to posts

  1. Stream in OBS

    PostDeck reads Kick and Twitch chat. When chat spikes, it saves the OBS replay and marks the recording.

  2. Make the clips

    OpenShorts picks the best moments, crops them to 9:16 and adds captions. PostDeck can remove the music and cut the silence.

  3. Review the clips

    Every clip waits on one screen with its title, tags and networks filled in. Press A to keep a clip and D to drop it.

  4. Schedule in one click

    PostDeck puts each kept clip into your next free posting time, then posts it.

Tools in the app

Live clipper
Saves a clip when chat reacts, and skips the waiting scenes.
Studio
The OpenShorts editor inside PostDeck, for edit styles, hooks and captions.
OBS dock
Save a clip, mark a moment, or turn on the live clipper from a panel in OBS.
Facecam cutout
Puts the camera over the gameplay in a vertical clip.
Scheduler
Posts at the time you set, while the app sits in the tray.
Analytics
Shows what you posted, where, and how each post did.
Stream VOD upload
Sends the full recording to YouTube, with chapters at the moments chat reacted.
Auto retry
Tries a failed post again, and starts with Windows so scheduled posts go out.

Who uses it

PostDeck is a personal tool. Only the owner of this copy uses it, to post to channels that the owner manages.

Use of Google and YouTube data

PostDeck asks Google for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and to read your channel name. It uses that permission only when you tell it to post. It stores the login token only on your computer. PostDeck's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
