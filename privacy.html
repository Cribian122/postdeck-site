PostDeck

Privacy policy

Last update: November 10, 2026

Summary

PostDeck is a desktop app. It keeps all data on your own computer. It does not send your data to us or to any other company, except to the social networks that you tell it to post to.

Data that PostDeck uses

  • Login tokens for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Bluesky. PostDeck gets them when you click Connect. It stores them on your computer, encrypted by Windows.
  • Account names, for example your channel name, to show which account is connected.
  • Your videos, titles and captions, which PostDeck uploads only when you post or schedule them.

How PostDeck uses the data

PostDeck uses this data only to upload your videos and posts to the accounts that you connected. It does not sell, share or analyze your data. It has no advertising and no tracking.

Google and YouTube

PostDeck uses YouTube API Services. When you connect YouTube, PostDeck asks for permission to upload videos and to read your channel name. By using PostDeck with YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of your data follows the Google Privacy Policy.

PostDeck's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other networks

When you post to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Bluesky, those companies receive the video and text under their own privacy policies.

How to remove your data

  • In PostDeck, open Profiles & accounts and click Disconnect to delete a login token.
  • To remove PostDeck's access to your Google account, open Google Account permissions and remove PostDeck.
  • To delete everything, uninstall PostDeck and delete the folder %APPDATA%\postdeck.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page shows the new date at the top.
